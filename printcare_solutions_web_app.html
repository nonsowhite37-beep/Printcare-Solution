About Our Company

PRINTCARE SOLUTIONS is a professional technical service and office-equipment support company specializing in printer and photocopier repairs, D.I. machine repairs, computer installations, general maintenance, consumables contracting, and the supply of printer consumables such as toners, drums and blades.
